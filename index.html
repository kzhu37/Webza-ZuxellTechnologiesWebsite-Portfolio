Meet Our Founder

A Vision Built on Precision

"Every optical system tells a story of physics, engineering, and purpose. At Zuxell, we make sure that story ends with exceptional performance."

Dr. Kenji Tanaka founded Zuxell Technologies in 2009 after a distinguished 12-year career at Canon's Advanced Optics Division and Hamamatsu Photonics. With a Ph.D. in Optical Sciences from the University of Arizona and over 18 patents in lens design and photonics integration, Dr. Tanaka has become a recognized authority in custom optical system development.

Under his leadership, Zuxell has delivered optical solutions for autonomous vehicles, space-based imaging, AR/VR headsets, and next-generation medical endoscopes — earning the trust of industry leaders worldwide.

Dr. Tanaka is a frequent keynote speaker at SPIE Photonics West and the Optical Society's annual conference. He serves on the advisory board of two photonics startups and mentors graduate students at Stanford's Ginzton Laboratory.

Our Expertise

Deep Knowledge Across Optical Disciplines

Our team brings specialized expertise in the most demanding areas of optical science and photonics engineering. With over 150 combined years of experience, we tackle challenges that others turn away from.

Custom Lens Design

Multi-element lens systems designed from first principles using Zemax OpticStudio, Code V, and proprietary optimization tools for UV through LWIR wavelengths. We specialize in aspheric, diffractive, and freeform surfaces to achieve performance that conventional spherical designs cannot match.

Zemax Code V UV–LWIR Freeform

Illumination Engineering

LED, laser, and broadband source integration with freeform optic design for uniform illumination in medical, industrial, and consumer applications. We use non-sequential ray tracing with millions of rays to predict and optimize irradiance distributions with sub-percent uniformity.

LED Freeform Medical Non-sequential

Opto-Mechanical Systems

Integrated mechanical housing design with thermal analysis, tolerance stack-ups, and vibration modeling for ruggedized field deployment. Our SolidWorks and ANSYS workflows ensure your optical assembly survives MIL-STD shock and vibe testing the first time.

SolidWorks ANSYS MIL-STD Thermal

Photonics & Opto-Electronics

Sensor fusion, photodetector circuit design, fiber-optic coupling, and signal processing for autonomous, medical, and telecom platforms. From InGaAs APDs to silicon photomultipliers, we select and integrate the right detector for your SNR, bandwidth, and cost targets.

InGaAs Fiber Optic Telecom SiPM

Imaging & Machine Vision

High-resolution camera system development, wavefront sensing, MTF optimization, and computational imaging for industrial inspection and surveillance. We co-design optics and ISP algorithms so your final image quality exceeds what either discipline achieves alone.

MTF Wavefront ISP Machine Vision

Optical Testing & Metrology

Interferometric measurement, stray light analysis, environmental stress testing, and ISO-compliant performance validation for production lenses. Our in-house lab features a Zygo interferometer, MTF bench, and environmental chambers for testing from -40°C to +85°C.

Zygo Stray Light ISO 10110 Environmental

Our Services

End-to-End Optical Solutions

We support your project through every stage — from feasibility analysis and optical simulation to prototyping and volume manufacturing support. Each engagement is tailored to your technical requirements, timeline, and budget.

Feasibility Studies & Concept Design

We evaluate optical performance requirements, identify physical and manufacturing constraints, and propose architecture options before committing to detailed design. Typical deliverables include a trade study report, preliminary ray trace models, and a project roadmap.

Request a Study

Zemax & Code V Optical Modeling

Sequential and non-sequential ray tracing, tolerancing, ghost analysis, and custom DLL scripting for complex multi-surface designs. We also perform stray light modeling using FRED and LightTools for critical space and defense applications.

Learn More

Prototyping & Rapid Development

Diamond-turned and molded prototype lenses, 3D-printed opto-mechanical housings, and benchtop testing to validate your design in weeks. Our network of precision fabricators across Asia and North America ensures fast turnaround with full traceability.

Learn More

Manufacturing & Quality Support

Lens drawing packages per ISO 10110, vendor selection and qualification, incoming inspection criteria, and on-site production support for high-volume optical assemblies. We bridge the gap between design intent and factory reality.

Learn More

LiDAR & Depth Sensing Systems

Custom scanning optics, time-of-flight receiver design, and beam steering solutions for automotive, robotics, and geospatial mapping. We've designed receiver and transmitter optics for both 905nm and 1550nm LiDAR platforms operating at ranges exceeding 200m.

Learn More

Laser Optics & Beam Delivery

High-power laser focusing, beam shaping, homogenization, and fiber launch optics for industrial cutting, medical ablation, and communications. We design for power densities up to 10 kW/cm² with appropriate thermal management and coating specifications.

Learn More

Meet Our Founder

A Vision Built on Precision

"Every optical system tells a story of physics, engineering, and purpose. At Zuxell, we make sure that story ends with exceptional performance."

Yang Lan founded Zuxell Technologies in 2009 after a distinguished 12-year career at Canon's Advanced Optics Division and Hamamatsu Photonics. With a Ph.D. in Optical Sciences from the University of Arizona and over 18 patents in lens design and photonics integration, Yang has become a recognized authority in custom optical system development.

Under his leadership, Zuxell has delivered optical solutions for autonomous vehicles, space-based imaging, AR/VR headsets, and next-generation medical endoscopes, earning the trust of industry leaders worldwide.

Yang is a frequent keynote speaker at SPIE Photonics West and the Optical Society's annual conference. He serves on the advisory board of two photonics startups and mentors graduate students at Stanford's Ginzton Laboratory.
